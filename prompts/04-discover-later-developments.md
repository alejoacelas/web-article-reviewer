# Discover later developments

Review the complete immutable article for verified qualifications and developments,
published after its latest substantive update, that would make a careful reader read a
passage differently. Collect the evidence; do not draft article text.

Return a finding when knowing the evidence would change what a reader takes a passage
to establish, whether the change is a qualification of one sentence or a reversal of a
section's conclusion. Do not decide the size of the fix; the drafter does.

Do not return narrow factual errors or unsupported citations (the targeted reviewers
own those), fresher examples or merely newer sources that leave the implication
unchanged, or developments that fit within the article's stated uncertainty. Prefer
primary sources, official datasets, and major systematic reviews. For each finding,
record the strongest reason an editor might reasonably leave the passage as it is.

Anchor every finding to the exact sentence whose reading changes; for a section-level
reversal, anchor to the sentence that states the conclusion and list the other affected
passages. Return every finding that meets the standard; do not target a particular
count. A later pass will judge each finding against the article's argument; when you
are unsure whether a development clears the threshold, return it and record the doubt
in the reason. Match `[OUTPUT_SCHEMA_JSON]`.
